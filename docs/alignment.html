OCTOBER 29, 2026 / TT CONTROL AUDIT

One pipeline.
From training to commands.

The official RoboMaster TT SDK, public TT/Tello projects and both contributors' implementation commits were compared. The goal stays fixed: recognize a wall print, center it, approach slowly and hold a measured stand-off.

01 / WHAT GOES INTO THE CONTROLLER

Pixels, features, actions.

YOLO locates the target. PID or PPO proposes movement. The same control gate checks every proposal.

  1. BGR camera frame → class, confidence and normalized box center/size.
  2. Three-frame target confirmation → vision, forward range, freshness and previous safe actions.
  3. 16 ordered features × 4 ticks → a 64-value input to PID or PPO.
  4. Two outputs, forward and yaw → supervision and measured arrival hold.
  5. Four SDK channels: rc 0 round(20 × forward) 0 round(25 × yaw).

Example: a supervised action of [0.4, −0.2] maps to rc 0 8 0 -5. RC values are stick/channel inputs, not calibrated meters per second. The starting control cadence is 10 Hz. Lateral and vertical policy outputs remain zero.

02 / DISCREPANCIES RESOLVED

Same inputs. Same stop rules.

Observation layout remains objectpilot-v1-16x4. Controller behavior is explicitly versioned as objectpilot-control-v2.

03 / EVIDENCE AND LIMITS

New behavior, separate evidence.

Software alignment does not establish physical flight performance.

The control-v2 PID diagnostic run completed 25 of 30 scenarios with zero simulated minimum-distance violations. These previously inspected seeds are development diagnostics, not a final test set. Changes to confirmation, command quantization and arrival verification mean the score must not be pooled with the older 28/30 or original 30/30 runs.

Windows and Linux CI each passed all 46 tests with no skips. The regression suite compares simulator and camera observations, commands and arrival step by step. CI also checks PPO train/save/load/evaluate/demo/replay with a nondefault calibration config. Short training checks validate software plumbing, not policy quality.

Actual TT video delay, forward sensor freshness, RC signs, gain, stopping drift and operator takeover remain hardware work. Grounded camera commands do not launch the aircraft.

The 324-candidate grid is a plateau above kp_forward 3.0; the tuned gains arrive faster and closer to the stand-off, overshoot it by up to 7 cm and change commands more. The PID improvement has overlapping intervals and is suggestive, not established. Ten times more PPO training helps every seed, but the best seed remains below both PID results and one seed keeps ending below the minimum clearance.

Three simulator findings that gains cannot fix are recorded for a team decision: detection dropout resets the two-second hold; the near-entry latch can fire about 5 cm beyond the ToF range and park the aircraft at 1.2 m; and the supervisor lets a policy creep past the minimum clearance because it protects the minimum, not the stand-off, without hysteresis, while the oblique range over-reads clearance and lag carries the aircraft on. All numbers are simulation results on uncalibrated response, not flight evidence.
